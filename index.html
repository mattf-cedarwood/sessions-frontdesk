Late checkout

Checkout is 11:00 AM. Two tiers. Offer it at check-in, not on checkout morning.

The whole dayLater Checkout (7 PM) $77Room until 7:00 PM. Cloudbeds item · SKU 036
The price is the price

$50 and $77 are what the guest pays. No tax is added. Never type a different price into Cloudbeds and never discount it. There is no earlier, cheaper tier any more — if you see an item that starts with RETIRED, don't use it.

Tell housekeeping

As soon as you sell one, tell housekeeping. A 1 PM room is cleaned later the same day. A 7 PM room is a hold-over and gets cleaned the next day.

What to say

“Checkout is at 11. If you'd like a slow morning, I can keep the room for you until 1 for $50 — or all the way to 7 tonight for $77, so you can get out and shower before the drive home.”

One more night

Offer every guest another night at half what they're paying — with a $100 minimum.

  • Price: half their nightly rate, rounded up to the dollar, never below $100.
  • Never a Saturday night. Don't offer it.
  • Only if their room is free that night (or you can move them to a free room of the same type).
$

Type their rate to see the offer.

Examples:

  • $120 a night → offer $100
  • $199 a night → offer $100
  • $201 a night → offer $101
  • $253 a night → offer $127

“Before you go — your room is free tomorrow night. If you'd like to stay one more night, I can do it for half what you're paying, [price]. Want me to add it?”

How to enter it — important

Extend the reservation by one night and set that night's rate to the offer price. Then type ONE MORE NIGHT in the reservation notes.

Never ring it up as a product or a custom charge. That charges the wrong tax and leaves the room open for an online booking on top of your guest.

Suggest what fits

Ask first, listen, then suggest one or two things that fit what they told you. Say the price once, then let it go — a “no” is fine.

How to charge it in Cloudbeds

The same five steps for everything on this page.

First time only

If the folio shows a green ADD/ADJUST CHARGE button instead of a blue + Add, click Switch to the new folio in the blue banner first.

  1. Open the reservation, click Folio

  2. Click + Add, then Product

    Not Room Revenue, not Adjustment. Product pulls from our item list.

  3. Pick the product category

    Use Fee for late checkout, early check-in, pet fee and cold plunge. Use Additionals for the Romance Package, e-bike and wellness pass. Shop items have no category — just search the name.

  4. Choose the product — the price fills itself

    Start typing the name (for example checkout). Never type or change the price. If the price doesn't fill in, you picked the wrong item. More than one? Change Quantity.

  5. Payment method, then Add

    Taking a card now? Choose the card. Putting it on the room? Leave Do not collect payment.

Why it matters

Every charge is credited to whoever is logged in. A custom charge, or ringing something through the wrong item, means the sale doesn't count as yours and our numbers go wrong.

Check yourself

Answer before you open each one.

A guest checks in Friday. Someone arrives into that exact room Saturday. Can you sell the 7 PM checkout?

Only if another room of the same room type is free Saturday night, so the arriving guest can be moved. Otherwise no.

A guest asks for late checkout "just until noon, for less". What do you say?

There is no noon tier. The options are 1 PM for $50 or 7 PM for $77. Never type a lower price into Cloudbeds.

How much tax goes on the 7 PM checkout?

None. $77 is the full price.

A guest wants One More Night for a Saturday. What is the price?

You don't offer it. One More Night is never a Saturday night.

How do you enter One More Night?

Extend the reservation by one night, set that night's rate, and type ONE MORE NIGHT in the notes. Never as a product.

A guest wants a beer bucket. What do you ring up?

There is no beer item in Cloudbeds at Sessions. Ask your manager — never invent a custom charge.

The price field is blank after you picked a product. What went wrong?

You picked the wrong item or didn't select it from the list. Pick again. Never type a price in.
